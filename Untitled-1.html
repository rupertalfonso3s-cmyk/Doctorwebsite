About MindCare Match

We help patients connect to the right care by combining mental health and general health needs in one guided process.

Our website makes it easier to understand what kind of support you need and which doctors can help most effectively. After answering a brief survey, patients receive a personalized list of care options, including doctor specialties, treatment goals, and scheduling availability.

  • Quick self-check for mental health and general well-being
  • Doctor recommendations based on patient needs
  • Availability and appointment scheduling overview
  • Support for stress, anxiety, sleep, and physical wellness
